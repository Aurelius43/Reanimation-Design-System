## Guidance

iOS bottom toolbar reference: Standard (Share, Add, More) or Editing (Delete, Duplicate, Done) context. On iPad the toolbar spans its column without the phone's bottom safe area.

`editing`, `sizeClass`. 44pt targets spaced evenly, glyphs in `icon-interactive`, 34pt bottom safe area. Every icon has an accessible name.
