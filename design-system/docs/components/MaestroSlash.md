## Guidance

The Maestro Slash: a controlled directional delimiter in Micro, Section, Hero and Motion variants.

`variant`, `width`. Toxic (`mutation-live`) bodies; Hero layers a creative purple body over a 24% toxic echo; Motion travels in once with `motion-hero` and the expressive curve (static under reduced motion). Use one slash per composition as a section break or hero gesture — never as a background pattern or bullet.
